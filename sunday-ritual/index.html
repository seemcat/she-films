sunday
ritual

every sunday, we show up to become the type of person who creates and posts.

the problem

you have 5,000+ videos and photos you'll "edit later." essays sitting in your substack drafts. ideas that never make it out of your notes app.

the problem isn't inspiration. it's execution.

sunday ritual is where we execute.

how it works

  • step 1. we meet to check in (via show & tell): every sunday at 7am pacific, in small groups
  • step 2. we connect & commit: we say out loud what we're posting this week
  • step 3. we work: on our craft during the week, tracked in poh
  • step 4. we reflect: a weekly reflection in poh before sunday
  • step 5. we prepare: one slide with what i posted, my high & lowlight, and what i'm posting next week

the commitment

  • • be live for at least 8 of the 11 sundays (7am pacific, camera on)
  • • post something every week
  • • show up for your acc-bestie (you get a new one every month)

rules for this season

  • rule 1. our priority is posting, not perfection.
  • rule 2. we build a habit of working on our craft.
  • rule 3. we create & post to discover our artistic selves.
  • rule 4. we're not falling off for at least 10 weeks.

who this is for

you're a girlie in your storytelling era. you film, you write, you make things. then you hoard them.

you'll thrive here if you:

  • ♡ make videos, essays, photos, or anything else you want to share online
  • ♡ value storytelling over chasing metrics
  • ♡ want to romanticize your creative process
  • ♡ are ready to commit to showing up weekly
  • ♡ actually want this (not just the idea of it)
