sunday
ritual

every sunday, we show up to become the type of person who creates and posts.

the problem

you have 5,000+ videos and photos you'll "edit later." essays sitting in your substack drafts. ideas that never make it out of your notes app.

the problem isn't inspiration. it's execution.

sunday ritual is where we execute.

how it works

every sunday we check in, connect, and commit to our creative dreams.

during the week, you work on your craft. you pick when and how often. almost too easy is the right size.

before sunday, you reflect on your week. then we gather for show-and-tell in small groups, and you leave having said out loud what you're posting next.

the commitment:

  • • show up every sunday
  • • work on your craft, and drop the proof on poh
  • • do your weekly reflection before every sunday
  • • post your drafts
  • • show up for your acc-bestie (you get a new one every month)
  • • bring supportive energy

who this is for

you're a girlie in your storytelling era. you film, you write, you make things. then you hoard them.

you'll thrive here if you:

  • ♡ make videos, essays, photos, or anything else you want to share online
  • ♡ value storytelling over chasing metrics
  • ♡ want to romanticize your creative process
  • ♡ are ready to commit to showing up weekly
  • ♡ actually want this (not just the idea of it)

how to join

season 3: oct 11 - dec 20

we kick off on oct 11, then show up for 10 sundays together at 7am pacific. graduation day is dec 20.

alumni — you're in for free. you earned your spot.

new members — $100. that's $10/week to build the habit of showing up for your creative dreams with a community of women who get it.

what you get:

  • ♡ sundays at 7am pacific — show-and-tell in small groups, and you leave every week having said out loud what you're posting
  • ♡ lifetime membership to she films society — the girls, the group chat, and every season after this one
  • ♡ lifetime access to proof of habit (poh) ($169.99 value) — where your weekly, monthly and seasonal reflections live
  • ♡ 3 member-led workshops (oct, nov, dec) — the first two are chosen from what the group is actually struggling with. december is the vision to action workshop to close out the year

the process:

  • • register — complete the application by oct 10
  • • wait — application review happens
  • • prepare — you'll receive an email with next steps
